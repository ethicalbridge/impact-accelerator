Why Handova

Willing hands on one side. Real needs on the other.

Around the world, skilled people want to help. But a volunteer role or an internship asks for more time than they have, and they rarely know which organisation needs them. Some give money; many would rather give what they know, a few hours a month. Meanwhile, locally led organisations carry big missions with small teams. Handova, an initiative of Ethical Bridge, builds the bridge between them.
