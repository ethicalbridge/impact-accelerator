Why Handova

Help that leaves, or capability that stays.

Well-meant volunteering often starts from what the helper wants to give. Handova starts from what the organisation needs, and ends with something that stays on both sides: capability for the team, a footprint for the professional.

Without a clear handover
  • Help shaped around the helper
  • Unscoped, open-ended favours
  • Access before trust
  • Nothing to show for it
  • Hard to get real experience
With Handova
  • The organisation defines the need and the output
  • One clear output in up to 16 hours
  • Approval first, a signed agreement before any access
  • Hours and outcomes reviewed by the organisation
  • An impact CV built from reviewed work

How it works

Five steps, one handover.

See the full process
Step 1 of 5

The organisation writes the need.

An approved organisation publishes one specific need: the challenge, the output it will keep, the skills and working languages, and a realistic number of hours.

  • The output is named before anyone applies
  • Up to three open needs at a time
  • No direct contact with children or adults at risk
Step 2 of 5

The right person steps forward.

Professionals apply with a short plan for the output, or the organisation invites an approved professional whose work fits. We can also introduce people by hand.

  • Applications explain how, not just who
  • Only approved profiles can apply
  • Either side can end a conversation
Step 3 of 5

Nothing starts until both sign.

Every match gets its own contribution agreement, built from the need: scope, timing, access, safeguarding and confidentiality.

  • Contact details shared only after both signatures
  • Never passwords, card details or access codes
  • Signatures are final and dated
Step 4 of 5

A short, protected contribution.

The professional delivers the agreed output, logs time as they go and keeps conversations on the platform. The organisation reviews each entry.

  • At most 16 hours
  • Remote only
  • Hours count only once reviewed
Step 5 of 5

The work stays with the team.

The output is handed over so the team can use it without help. The organisation writes an endorsement, and the professional chooses to add it to their impact CV. Six months later, we ask if it is still in use.

  • A handover the team can run alone
  • An endorsement in the organisation’s words
  • A six-month “still in use?” check

Questions

Good to know before you start.

Is it really free?

Yes. Handova is free for organisations and for professionals.

How is Handova linked to Ethical Bridge?

Handova is an initiative of Ethical Bridge. Organisations in the Ethical Bridge directory can use it to get extra support from skilled professionals, free.

Is the work paid?

No. Contributions are voluntary. Any expenses need separate written terms between the two of you.

Who can join?

Locally led organisations listed in the Ethical Bridge directory, registered or fiscally hosted, and professionals aged 18 or over, anywhere in the world.

How long is a contribution?

Every need takes at most 16 hours, agreed up front and logged as you go.

How much time do professionals commit?

At least 4 hours a month, and each professional chooses how many. To keep the community active and save organisations’ time, a profile is marked inactive after three months without activity, such as applying to a need, and closed after six. We send an email two weeks before each step, and a closed profile can be published again at any time, after we review it.

What does the professional get?

Real experience and a reviewed footprint on their impact CV: what they did, for whom, and the organisation’s endorsement in its own words. It is an exchange where both sides keep something.

Why the name Handova?

It comes from “hand over”: the moment a piece of work passes to the team that keeps it. Every engagement ends with a handover.

What if something goes wrong?

Either side can pause or end a conversation or engagement, and anyone can report a concern. Our safeguarding lead reviews every report.
