What is Impact Accelerator?

Impact Accelerator is a structured talent-to-project platform connecting skilled people with real, clearly scoped needs from organisations working for social and environmental good.

Every contribution has agreed outcomes, an hours commitment, project support, review and verified recognition.

Connected to Ethical Bridge

Impact Accelerator is part of the broader Ethical Bridge ecosystem.

Ethical Bridge maps and verifies organisations. Impact Accelerator builds on that trusted network by connecting these organisations with the talent they need to strengthen their work.

The platforms share commitments to ethical practice, safeguarding and transparency, while Impact Accelerator provides its own talent, projects and contribution experience.
